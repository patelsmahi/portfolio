Sonar

Sonar is a conceptual music discovery app designed to simplify how users find new music that matches their taste.

Problem Statement: Music discovery often feels overwhelming, with existing algorithms lacking personalization and making it difficult for users to find songs that genuinely resonate. To address this, I designed Sonar, an interactive prototype that makes music discovery more engaging and personal. Using a familiar swipe interaction, users can listen to short previews and quickly decide what they like. Liked songs are automatically saved to a playlist that can be exported to their preferred streaming platform.

Role: UI/UX Designer
Duration: 1 Month

Project Overview & Outcome

Sonar is an interactive music discovery app that allows users to explore new songs through a swipe-based interface. The app provides a personalized experience by enabling users to like or dislike songs, which are then saved to a playlist for easy access and export to their preferred streaming platform. The design and feel is heavily inspired by the popular dating app, Tinder, which uses a similar swipe interaction to engage users. The goal of Sonar is to make music discovery more intuitive, enjoyable, and tailored to individual tastes.

What my idea is: I want to create a fun and interactive music discovery app that lets users explore new songs through a swipe-based interface, which includes liking or disliking tracks to build a playlist that can be exported directly to their preferred streaming service. The app also includes song previews, artist information, and genre-based recommendations to make discovery more personalized.

Outcome: I designed and prototyped Sonar in Figma, creating a visually appealing, user-friendly interface inspired by Tinder's swipe interaction. The goal was to make music discovery more intuitive and enjoyable. The final prototype lets users discover new music, build personalized playlists, and share their favorite songs with friends.

Understanding Audience


User Research & Insights

To understand the needs and preferences of potential users, I conducted basic user research to gather information. The survey aimed to gather insights on how users discover new music, their frustrations with current methods, and what features they would like in a music discovery app. The findings revealed that users often feel overwhelmed by the sheer volume of music available and desire a more personalized and engaging way to explore new tracks.

Process


Design Goals

The main design goal was to improve the user experience by implementing a light mode option, allowing users to switch between light and dark themes based on their preferences and lighting conditions. This update aimed to enhance accessibility, readability, and overall usability of the app for all students navigating the campus.


  • Enhance readability and comfort

    Ensure text, icons, and interface elements are easy to read in a bright environment.

  • Maintain brand consistency

    Adapt the existing dark mode style while keeping the app’s visual identity intact.

  • Support accessibility standards

    Ensure sufficient contrast and usability for all users, including those with visual impairments

  • Provide a seamless toggle experience

    Allow users to switch between dark and light mode without disrupting their workflow or navigation.
