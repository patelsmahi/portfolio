Jury-X Redesign

Jury-X is a jury research and consulting firm that helps legal teams analyze juror profiles, identify bias, and inform trial strategy through data driven insights. When evaluating their website, I found that key information was difficult to access despite the brand’s strong authority. The navigation was complex, making it unclear who the team was, what services were offered, and how potential clients could get started.

Problem Statement: Potential clients need a clear and efficient way to understand Jury-X’s services and expertise, but the existing website structure makes important information difficult to find and navigate.

Outcome: In my redesign, I focused on creating a more intuitive and transparent experience. I simplified the navigation, prioritized essential content, and strengthened the visual hierarchy to better communicate Jury-X’s expertise.

Role: UI/UX Designer
Duration: 3 Months

Project Overview & Outcome

This redesign project was assigned as part of a UI/UX design course at UNC Chapel Hill. The goal was to analyze the existing Jury-X website, identify key pain points, and create a more intuitive and user friendly design that better communicates the company’s expertise and services. I conducted a heuristic evaluation of the current site, gathering insights on navigation issues, content organization, and visual hierarchy. Conducting early user testing with potential users helped me understand their needs and frustrations, which informed my design decisions.By following a user centered design process, I aimed to enhance the overall user experience and make it easier for potential clients to understand what Jury-X offers and how to get started.

Key Issue: Jury-X’s existing website had a complex navigation structure that made it difficult for users to find essential information about the company’s services, team, and expertise. This lack of clarity hindered potential clients from fully understanding what Jury-X offers and how to engage with their services. For a company that relies heavily on trust and credibility, this was a significant barrier to user engagement and conversion.

Design Outcome: In my redesign, I focused on simplifying the navigation and improving the visual hierarchy to make key information more accessible. I created a clearer structure that prioritized essential content such as services offered, team members, contact information, and pricing. By streamlining the user flow and enhancing the overall aesthetics, the redesigned website provides a more intuitive and engaging experience for potential clients, ultimately helping to build trust and encourage them to take action.

Understanding The Audience


User Testing/Interviews

To better understand the needs and frustrations of potential users, I conducted early user testing sessions with individuals who fit the target audience profile. These sessions revealed that users struggled to find key information about Jury-X’s services and expertise, which made it difficult for them to trust the company and consider engaging with their offerings. This insight highlighted the importance of simplifying the navigation and improving the overall clarity of the website to better communicate Jury-X’s value proposition.



Audience Analysis

More in depth audience analysis revealed target audience. I looked into demographics of potential clients, which primarily included legal professionals.



Persona Creation & Empathy Mapping

Based on the user testing and audience analysis, I created detailed personas that represented the key characteristics, needs, and pain points of potential clients. Creating an empathy map allowed for further analysis on users. For this project, I focused on Efficinet Ella, who represents a legal professional looking for efficient and trustworthy jury research services.



2

Redesign Structure


Card Sort & Information Hierarchy

To address the navigation issues identified in the user testing, I conducted a card sorting exercise to better understand how users would categorize and prioritize the information on the website. This exercise revealed that users expected a more streamlined structure that grouped related content together and made it easier to find essential information about services, team members, and contact details.




User Flows

User flows helped visualize the key paths that users would take to access important information and complete desired actions on the website. These flows helped identify potential pain points and areas for improvement in the navigation structure, ensuring that users could easily find what they were looking for and understand how to engage with Jury-X’s services.

User Flow 1: Book a Trial Potential clients can quickly book a trial by clicking the “Contact Us” button in the navigation or hero section, then accessing the “Book Your Trial” contact form.

User Flow 2: Contact a Team Member Potential clients can visit the “About Us” page to view team profiles, contact information, and connect directly with team members. They can also use the “Contact Us” page for general inquiries and team contact options



Low-Fidelity Wireframes

Low-fidelity wireframes to explore different layout options and design solutions for the redesigned website. These wireframes focused on simplifying the navigation, improving the visual hierarchy, and ensuring that key information was easily accessible to users. The wireframes served as a foundation for the high-fidelity prototype and helped for early feedback and iteration on the overall structure and user experience of the website.

Final Updates


UI Kit

Creating an UI kit helped establish consistent visuals and brand identity. The kit includes a color palette, typography, and design elements that align with Jury-X's brand. This served as a guide for the high-fidelity prototype and ensured that the final design was cohesive and visually appealing.



High-Fidelity Wireframes & Prototype

High-fidelity wireframes incorporated visuals elements to the final design, creating a polished and realistic representation of the redesigned website. With colors, typography, and imagery, the high-fidelity prototype mirrored a final product that would be used for user testing and feedback.




User Testing & Design Iteration

User testing through usertesting.com allowed for gathering feedback on the user experience of the redesign. This feedback helped identify areas for improvement and informed design iterations to enhance the overall usability and effectiveness of the website. By incorporating user feedback, I was able to refine the design and ensure that it effectively met the needs of potential clients while also aligning with Jury-X’s brand and goals.
Key Insights:
- CTA visibility: Users had difficulty finding the “Book Your Trial” button, which was a key call to action for potential clients.
- Transparency: Users wanted clearer, upfront information about the services offered, pricing, and about the team.
- Contact clarity: Users expected easier access to team member information, which is crucial for building trust and credibility
Design Changes:
- Moved the “Book Your Trial” CTA higher on the Services page to improve visibility
- Improved the clarity and transparency of service information to better communicate what Jury-X offers
- Made team members more visible and clickable to enhance trust and credibility
